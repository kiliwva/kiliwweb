Features

Everything a cloud should do.

No clutter, no dark patterns — just fast, private storage that respects your time.

Share in one click

Public links that work without an account — with optional passwords, or restricted to exactly the people you choose.

Work together

Give teammates edit access to any folder by email. Whole folders download as a single ZIP, streamed instantly.

Photos & previews

A gallery of every photo you own, live thumbnails for images and videos, previews for PDFs, audio and more.

Undo your mistakes

Deleted files rest in the trash for 30 days before they're gone. Restore in one click, exactly where they were.

Private by default

Two-factor sign-in, per-device sessions you can revoke, and automatic screening that keeps shared content safe.

Built for developers

A clean REST API with keys, hotlink URLs for embedding, presigned browser uploads and signed webhooks — build on your storage.

Pricing

Simple plans, fair prices.

30-day periods, no auto-renew surprises. Switching plans converts your unused days into time on the new one.
